Why SWAFAA Was Created

The Problem

  • Lack of transparency and accountability
  • Decisions without member input
  • Financial mismanagement concerns
  • Retaliation and silence
  • Disconnect from the rank-and-file

Our Solution

SWAFAA is building a new independent union with:

  • Real term limits
  • Full financial transparency
  • Direct member control
  • Safeguards against corruption
  • Member-focused Constitution & Bylaws

What We're Building

A grassroots movement laying the foundation for a certified union that truly represents Southwest Flight Attendants.

Progress So Far

  • SWAFAA Organizing Group, LLC formed in Texas
  • EIN secured with IRS
  • Constitution & Bylaws in final review
  • Growing member support
