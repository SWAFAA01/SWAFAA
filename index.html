Why SWAFAA?

Because Flight Attendants Deserve a Choice

SWAFAA was created because we believe Flight Attendants deserve something better: an organization built for the members, by the members, and accountable to the people it serves.

This is About You

SWAFAA isn't about one person telling everyone else what to do. It's about creating an opportunity for Flight Attendants to learn, ask questions, consider their options, and ultimately decide for themselves what kind of representation they want.

You deserve to have a choice.

We're asking you to be informed. We're asking you to look at what's possible and decide for yourself whether this is something you want to support.

This is about making sure you have a choice.

Our Mission

SWAFAA is a grassroots movement created by and for working Flight Attendants. We are building member-led, transparent, and focused on what you actually need.

This is about choice under the Railway Labor Act. Flight Attendants have the right to organize and choose their own representative. The National Mediation Board runs fair elections. Authorization cards are just what gets us to a vote — they are not the vote itself.

The Authorization Card

The card is simple. By signing, you're requesting the opportunity to vote. That's it. One signature. One choice. No obligations. No changes to your contract.
